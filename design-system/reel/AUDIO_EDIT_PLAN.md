# Audio edit plan — cue-linked, lock after recording

The voice owns timing. These placements are a score for the editor, not already-existing sounds or measured voice onsets.

| Beat | Track action | Trigger / envelope | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | VO starts immediately; quiet room tone underneath | First consonant <=150 ms. No loud pre-roll sting. | Hook words fully intelligible. |
| Card lands | One dry card/table contact | E02 contact frame, align onset within 1 frame. Audition sfx_card for fit; it may be a flip, not a slide. | If wrong material, omit or source/create a suitable tactile sound; don't mislabel the file. |
| Online | Music bed begins gently | Start near C03, 250–400 ms fade. | No volume jump or branded stock jingle. |
| Night | Optional quiet night cue | C05 start, no more than the useful tail. | Not a horror scream or a wall of reverb. |
| Day | Thin music/room shift | C08 «الصبح», ~200 ms envelope. | No new competing melody. |
| Vote reaction | Music -4..-6 dB relative to its current bed | Begin before C11 question, hold through بتشكركم, recover after phrase. | Dry joke gets room; no laugh track. |
| Offline | Keep same bed | No extra sound just because a new feature is named. | Continuous energy. |
| Pivot | Brief music dip then warm room tone | C13 invitation → C14 identity cut. | Voice remains the same person. |
| Case folder | One paper placement/unfold cue | Folder landing after E10. | Doesn't mask قهوة المعلم. |
| Limited moves | At most one small paper/wood tick | At the illustrated selection/decrement, not every meter cell. | Human-scale, not arcade money sounds. |
| Three questions | Voice with little or no foley | C17; question text follows words. | Viewer hears each question clearly. |
| Closing | Resolve bed, preserve clean hold | After C19 last word, fade 0.6–1 s during final hold. | No early fade of final consonant, no black/audio tail. |

No new music or SFX was generated during preparation. Candidate files are copied locally and their metadata is in PREPARATION_REPORT.md. Audition before use. Sound suitability cannot be inferred from filenames.

Edit non-VO audio to cue timing with fades/crossfades at meaningful boundaries; preserve VO native speed. Never put editing instructions, music or foley tags into the TTS script. Export narration separately from the final mix so later changes remain cheap.
