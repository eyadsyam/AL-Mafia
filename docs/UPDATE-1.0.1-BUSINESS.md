# Update 1.0.1 — business assumptions (not income promises)

Owner's goal: a sustainable Egyptian-first online Mafia business with enjoyable repeat play. Ads reward chosen moments, not minutes spent idle. Optional digital purchases fund cosmetic expression; never sell votes, role knowledge, protection, extra night actions, or competitive strength.

## Proposed launch offer sheet
- Council Pass: permanent removal of automatic interstitial interruptions. Planning price EGP150 within owner's EGP150-200 range; voluntary rewarded ads remain. Localized Play price is authoritative. No subscription, hidden renewal or fake markdown.
- Coin packs:500/1200/2500. Suggested EGP29.99/59.99/119.99 pending Console regional-price review; prices are unconfigured proposals, never hardcoded promises in the app.
- Daily20; free wheel expected23.8; seven collected days bonus60 with no missed-day penalty. Passive daily economy about366.6/week excluding match earnings. Existing catalog prices must stay legible so earliest meaningful item is attainable after normal play, while larger collections remain goals.
- Store15actual cosmetic SKUs; all must buy/equip/render. Threepack covers and hero are NOT additional cosmetic products. No invented items to pad variety.

## Revenue model
Monthly gross ads =30 * DAU * [rewarded served impressions/user/day * rewarded eCPM + interstitial served impressions/user/day * interstitial eCPM]/1000.
All quantities measured after opt-in, completion, fill, frequency limits and ad-free ownership. Never assume3ads/match. Longer sessions alone earn nothing.
