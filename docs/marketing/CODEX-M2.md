- A 4.5 rating is a goal, not an input you can guarantee.
- The neutral review trigger improves review quality but should not be modelled as a known ranking lift.
- Replying to reviews supports trust and recovery; it does not independently create meaningful discovery.

## 2. Recomputed middle case

Starting point:

- **4,300 installs**
- **475 active devices/day**
- **$27.5 ads + $6.4 in-app purchases = $33.9**

Accepted additions:

- N1 store organic: **+300 installs**
- N2 organizer groups: **+250 installs**
- N3: **no baseline increase**
- Total: **4,850 installs**

Using the current 0.110 active-device/install relationship:

- **≈536 active devices/day**
- Gulf middle share 5%: **27 Gulf devices**
- Egypt: **509 devices**

Revenue calculation:

- Egypt: 509 × $0.0715 ≈ **$36.4**
- Gulf planning value: ads at 3× Egypt plus unchanged conservative in-app-purchase assumption ≈ $0.1875/device/month
- Gulf: 27 × $0.1875 ≈ **$5.1**
- **Recomputed middle total: ≈$41.5**

Breakdown is approximately:

- **Ads: $34–35**
- **In-app purchases: $7**
- **Total: ≈$41–42**

Even the favorable non-breakout version—900 store installs, 500 organizer installs and 8% Gulf share—lands around **630 active devices/day and $49–50 total**.

Therefore, **$100 is not reachable without a major distribution event or roughly tripling the middle active-device base**. The model needs around 1,400 active devices/day, which at current retention means approximately **12,500–13,000 month-one installs**.

The single most important lever is not metadata, timing or Gulf mix. It is a **repeatable creator-hosted acquisition network measured by completed matches**. To close the gap without depending on an algorithmic breakout, the plan needs something closer to:

- 20–30 creator-hosted sessions rather than 3–6;
- average 250–400 attributable installs per creator cohort, or an equivalent combination of installs and materially better retention;
- rehearsed browser joining so creator audiences actually complete a match;
- reusable clips from every session feeding the normal content system.

That is still difficult, but it is controllable outreach and event distribution rather than “hope one short reaches 500K.”

Store organic remains worth adding because it compounds every other channel. N2 is worth doing because it reaches people already experiencing the narrator problem. Gulf segmentation improves the value of acquired devices. None closes a $66 gap alone.
