# Marketing context — glossary

The words the marketing plan uses, so a post, a video brief and a creator pitch all
mean the same thing by them. Glossary only; decisions live in `DECISIONS.md`.

## The game

**القعدة (Table mode)** — the offline game: one phone passed around 6–15 people in one
room; the phone is the narrator. Never call it "offline mode" in public copy — say
what it does ("من غير راوي").

**الأوضة (Online room)** — an online match with voice. *Private room*: opened by a host
and shared by link/code with people they know. *Public room*: listed for strangers to
join. The two are marketed differently; "online" alone is ambiguous.

**قضية اليوم (Case of the Day)** — the daily fictional whodunit inside the app (1.1).
One accusation, limited tries, streaks, a shareable grid.

**القضية الأولى (the First Case)** — the Case of the Day on L-day. It carries the answer
to Season 1's finale.

**شريكك (Partner)** — the character a player picks in the app (1.1). In public copy it is
always «شريكك», never "team". A **side** is the group of viewers who picked the same
partner (four sides); sides are plural, the partner is personal.

**Founder seal (الختم الأول)** — the one lifetime pre-registration reward: a founder title
plus a sealed letter that opens on first launch with an extra clue to «القضية الأولى».

## The cast

**الراوي (the Narrator)** — the brand's voice and the only character who speaks aloud.
Hosts, reads comments and names, never takes sides.

**The Four (the Card cast)** — the four painted role characters from the app: the Reaper
(Mafia), the Doctor (white coat), the Detective, the Citizen. Faceless, grayscale. They
**write, never speak**: their lines appear as typed notes, in the app's own voice.

**الشلة (the Gang)** — the fixed cast of human AI characters at the table (Hamada, Karim,
Nada, Doctor Sayed, Uncle Reda). Carry the comedy through reaction stills; no lip-sync.

**Spoiler rule** — tier-1 bond lines, home lines and moment lines may be quoted in
marketing; tier-2 only partially; tier-3/4 lines and the letters are app-exclusive.

## Content

**Pillar (P1–P6)** — a kind of standalone short: P1 «اعرف اللعبة», P2 feature
spotlight, P3 «مواقف», P4 «مين المافيا؟», P5 trend-jack, P6 attachment.

**Cold case** — a standalone P4 case with no season-arc progress; can run indefinitely.

**Season 1** — the ~30-episode P4 arc from L-30 to L, whose finale is solved only in the app.

**Shot library** — the reusable set of ~80 generated motion shots every template draws
from, built once so each post needs little new footage.

**End card** — the 2–3 s real-app screen that closes every video (4–5 s in P2, if allowed).

**Creator room** — a room a partnered content creator hosts live so their audience
joins and plays with them. The main offer to creators.

## Numbers and phases

**Install** — a Google Play install. The leading indicator, not the goal.

**DAU** — daily active players. With ad eCPM, it is what month-1 income is made of.

**AdMob-alone target** — ≥ $100 of AdMob earnings in month 1.

**K (K-factor)** — new installs each installer brings: share rate × invites per sharer ×
invite conversion. The host-invite multiplier is 1 / (1 − K).

**T0** — the first day 12 opted-in testers are on the closed track.

**Phase A** — T0 to production access: no store destination; CTA «تابع».

**Phase B** — production access to L: pre-registration is the CTA.

**L-day** — launch day: 1.1 live in production on Google Play, always a Thursday. Plan
dates are offsets (L-7, L, L+3) until the real date is known.
