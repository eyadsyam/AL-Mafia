# Marketing context — glossary

The words the marketing plan uses, so a post, a video brief and a creator pitch all
mean the same thing by them. Glossary only; decisions live in `DECISIONS.md`.

**القعدة (Table mode)** — the offline game: one phone passed around 6–15 people in one
room; the phone is the narrator. Never call it "offline mode" in public copy — say
what it does ("من غير راوي").

**الأوضة (Online room)** — an online match with voice. *Private room*: opened by a host
and shared by link/code with people they know. *Public room*: listed for strangers to
join. The two are marketed differently; "online" alone is ambiguous.

**الراوي (the Narrator)** — the recurring AI character who is the brand's voice: old,
low-voiced, oil lamp. Tells stories and trailers. Not a player.

**الشلة (the Gang)** — the fixed cast of AI characters who appear in comedy videos,
each a recognisable Mafia-table archetype. Same faces every time.

**Creator room** — a room a partnered content creator hosts live so their audience
joins and plays with them. The main offer to creators.

**Install** — a Google Play install. The single number the launch month is judged on.

**The Card cast** — the four painted role characters from the app itself: the Reaper
(Mafia), the Citizen, the Detective, the Doctor. Faceless, grayscale, cinematic. Distinct
from the Gang, who are human and comic.

**L-day** — launch day, the day 1.0.1 is live on Play. Plan dates are written as offsets
(L-7, L, L+3) until the real date is known.
