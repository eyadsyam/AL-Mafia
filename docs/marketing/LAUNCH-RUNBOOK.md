# LAUNCH-RUNBOOK — Mafia Master · سيد المافيا

Dates and operations. Strategy in `PLAN-V3.md`, creative in `CONTENT-SYSTEM.md`, terms in
`CONTEXT.md`. All times Cairo. Nothing starts before «ابدأ شغل».

**Offsets:** **T0** = first day 12 opted-in testers are on the closed track (any 1.0.x
build counts). **A+n** = day n of Phase A. **L** = the Thursday 1.1 goes live.

**The gate [V]:** 12 testers for 14 continuous days → apply for production access
(review usually ≤ 7 days) → production **and** pre-registration open. Pre-registration
lasts ≤ 90 days per country; Google recommends 3–6 weeks before launch.

**L rule:** the earliest Thursday ≥ T+42, 1.1 approved under managed publishing, outside
Jan 3–22. Worked example [A]: T0 = Oct 12 → apply Oct 26 → access ≈ Nov 2 → earliest
Thursday ≥ T+42 is Nov 26, *if* 1.1 is approved by then; otherwise the next valid one
(Dec 17 is the reference date in `PLAN-V3.md` §4).

## 7. If 1.1 slips

- No date is announced before approval, so a slip costs momentum, not credibility.
- Season 1 starts only when L is ~30 ± 5 days away; until then, cold cases indefinitely.
- Episodes 25–29 are written as standalone cases so the arc can stretch ~10 days.
- Slip > 2 weeks after Season 1 started: pause the arc on a clean beat (not a cliffhanger); keep daily cases.
- Pre-registration nearing 90 days: launch or it is terminated in every country [V] — never open it early.
