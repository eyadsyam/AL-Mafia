# LAUNCH-RUNBOOK — Mafia Master · سيد المافيا

Dates and operations. Strategy in `PLAN-V3.md`, creative in `CONTENT-SYSTEM.md`, terms in
`CONTEXT.md`. All times Cairo. **Phase A starts only after this plan is frozen and the S0
art-consistency test passes on the app's canonical art, then «ابدأ شغل».** 1.1 is the first
public release.

**Offsets:** **T0** = first day 12 opted-in testers are on the closed track (any 1.0.x build
counts). **A+n** = day n of Phase A. **L** = the Thursday 1.1 goes live.

**The gate [V]:** 12 testers for 14 continuous days → apply for production access (review
usually ≤ 7 days) → production **and** pre-registration open. Pre-registration lasts ≤ 90
days per country; Google recommends 3–6 weeks before launch.

**L rule:** the earliest Thursday that passes every quality gate — ≥ T+42, 1.1 approved under
managed publishing, outside Jan 3–22. Worked example [A]: T0 = Oct 12 → apply Oct 26 →
access ≈ Nov 2 → earliest Thursday ≥ T+42 is Nov 26, *if* 1.1 is approved by then. Dec 17 is
the reference date in `PLAN-V3.md` §4.

**Feb 4 contingency.** If readiness lands in late January (after the exam window), the launch
is **Thursday Feb 4, 2027** — the strongest natural window, days before Ramadan. It is a
contingency for a late build, never a reason to hold a ready one.

**Volume throughout:** 7–10 primary shorts a week, each re-cut with a different hook per
platform; from L, creator-session clips on top.

|---|---|
| Installs < 1,200 | (1) move remaining boost to the best creator clip; (2) extra page-hosted private rooms (link shared, not a scheduled public table); (3) push the case notification hard |

non-Thursday sessions, the options are to cap sessions at 6 a week, or for the owner to name
someone as a second safety host — his call.
