**The number fell** from ~$41.5 / ~$60 (Round 9) to **~$27 / ~$41**. Three reasons: pre-registration

**$100 total needs** ~1,400 devices a day at base rates, ~1,250 with the Q4 lift ≈ 11,000–11,500
month-1 installs. **In month 1 that needs two breakouts** on top of the network. Without a
breakout, cumulative $100 arrives around **mid-to-late January 2027 with the network** and around
**late February (early Ramadan) without it** [E].

**What the plan does about it:**
1. **Unblock the date:** the 12 testers opted in by Oct 1 is P0 (`LAUNCH-RUNBOOK.md` §0). Adding confirmed creators as testers helps both the gate and the network.
2. **Run the creator network from this week** — the one lever we control (§3.1, `CREATORS.md`).
3. **Maximize breakout odds** with hook-first shorts from day 1 — real app footage and text in

4. **Protect device value:** normal AdMob serving in L-week, ads at «القعدة», retention.

## 2. The model

**Per 1,000 active devices per month, middle** [E, 1.1 spec]: Egypt ads ~$58 + IAP ~$13.5 →
~$71.5. Gulf ads ≈ 3× Egypt [E; UAE rewarded / interstitial eCPM $5.4 / $2.9 per Appodeal
2025 [V]]. Blended at 5% Gulf with the Q4 lift: ~$81 per 1,000 devices.

| Input | Low | Middle | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| eCPM rewarded / automatic / banner (Egypt) | $0.8 / 0.35 / 0.03 | $1.5 / 0.75 / 0.08 | $2.4 / 1.3 / 0.10 |
| Realized fill | 50% | 70% | 85% |
| Gulf share of devices | 3% | 5% | 8% |

- Device mix 60% local-table / 25% online-only / 15% mixed [E]; one table = one earning device.
- Retention D1 30%, D7 10–12%, D30 ~5% [A] → ~0.11 average devices per install in month 1 [E]; each D7 point ≈ +20% devices.
- K_install ≈ 0.04; no 1/(1−K) until cohorts prove it.
- Web joiners who never install are not devices. The creator entitlement removes automatic ads only on creators' own accounts — negligible in the model.

## 9. Risks and kill rules

| Risk | Rule |
|---|---|
| Testers not opted in by Oct 1 | L moves to Nov 5 or later; no public date before approval |
| Kratos voice published before its gates | nothing with Kratos audio ships until provenance, commercial-use authorization and the F16 listener gate are on record; week 1 is text + app footage |
| Limited ad serving in L-week | P0 checklist; link the moment the listing is live |
| No breakout | planning ~$41; say so; don't chase it with spend |
| Creator list unverified | nothing is sent from an unverified row |
| Creator sessions that don't finish matches | rehearsal + browser-join test first; completed matches are the metric |
| Real people on our pages | creator footage only as a credited share of the creator's own post (open decision) |
| AI-slop backlash | kill a format at ≥ 15% anti-AI comments or completion < 25% |
| AI labels | every video labelled AI |
| Hooks that arrive late | the hook audit (`CONTENT-SYSTEM.md` §5) at every approval |
| Targeting | never target religious identities |
| IP / real people | no names, footage or likeness of real people in our own content; no licensed music; no competitor names |
| Fiction vs product | stories and cases are fiction, never in-game clues; narrator audio only on public beats |
| Offer creep / unshipped features | rejected at approval |
| D1 < 20% | stop all spend, fix onboarding first |

**Kill / pivot:** formats survive at completion ≥ 30%, shares ≥ 3, follows ≥ 5 per 1k and
returning viewers ≥ 15% [A]. At **Oct 22** (L-7): followers < 1,000 across platforms → drop
drama, all-in on voice-led proofs, cases and creators. At **Nov 5** (L+7) → `LAUNCH-RUNBOOK.md` §5.
