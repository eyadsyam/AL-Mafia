# Connected Google Play screenshot campaign

The six Arabic phone screenshots in `store/screenshots/play-ar/` are generated
by `tool/generate_play_screenshots.py`. The phones contain real captures from
the online-enabled release APK. Only the surrounding panorama is generated art.

## Final generated background prompt

Mode: built-in image generation. Use case: `ads-marketing`.

> A very simple premium dark background that can connect six vertical
> screenshot panels side by side for an Arabic Mafia social deduction game.
> Nearly black charcoal paper texture, one thin antique-gold thread flowing
> gently from far left to far right, extremely faint soft smoke and a restrained
> deep burgundy glow only near the center. Abundant negative space; background
> only; no text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, phones, people, furniture,
> architecture, cards, symbols, objects or watermark.

Selected source: `raw_assets/store/connected-play-panorama.png`.
