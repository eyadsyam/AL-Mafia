ROLE
Read-only adversarial review of uncommitted Flutter client changes in the Mafia Master repo (a social-deduction party game, Arabic-first). Change NO files. Your deliverable is your final message.

WHAT TO REVIEW
Run `git diff -- lib test` and read every hunk, plus enough surrounding code to judge it. The changes:
1. lib/ui/screens/online/online_table_flow.dart
