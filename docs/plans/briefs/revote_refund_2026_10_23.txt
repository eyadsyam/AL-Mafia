BACKGROUND
«إعادة التصويت» (`revote`) is a paid table tool. A player spends a charge with the `economy` function action `toolUse` (SQL: the newest public.use_table_tool — find it with grep, the last definition in filename order is live). Spending decrements public.player_tool_charges.charges unless public.has_all_access(user) is true, then inserts a public.room_tool_uses row (tool_code 'revote', phase_number = the day). resolve_vote reads it with revoteRequested(toolUses, day) (supabase/functions/_shared/tools.ts) and ballotVerdict (supabase/functions/_shared/vote_rules.ts). Since today, a bought revote over a clear winner opens round 2 only when at least two seats drew votes (`narrowedCandidates`).
The owner's rule: an item a player paid for must really work. Today the charge is simply lost whenever the bought revote changes nothing.
