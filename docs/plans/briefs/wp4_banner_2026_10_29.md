# WP4: the banner never shifts the layout, plus one persistent shell banner behind a flag

First read the shared rules in `docs/plans/briefs/impl_common_2026_10_29.md`, the WP4 row of `docs/plans/UPDATE-1.5-PLAN.md`, and section `#u2` of `design-system/screens/update-1-5-mockups.html`. Before writing any UI, load these skills: `frontend-design`, `ui-ux-pro-max`, `animations`, `flutter-fix-layout-issues`.

## The owner's complaint
When the ad banner arrives, the whole screen jumps up. Each screen also loads its own banner again. He wants nothing to move, and ideally one banner that stays across screens.

## What exists today
- `lib/ui/economy/waiting_banner.dart` draws a `WaitingBanner` on these allowlisted waiting surfaces:
  - home
  - profile
  - history
  - vault/coin store
  - online hub (online_entry)
  - lobby (`belowPrimaryAction`)
  - stories hub
  - characters
  - deck_public
- `test/unit/banner_placement_test.dart` fails if the widget appears in any file outside its allowlist. Keep that guarantee.
- The slot is `_BannerSlot` in `lib/platform/monetization/rewarded_ads_mobile.dart` (~826-905). It has zero height until an ad fills, and that is the jump.
- Ad units come per placement from `bannerUnitFor` (~100-110). The package is google_mobile_ads 9.1.0. Once the AdWidget is disposed, the same BannerAd can be mounted again, one widget at a time.
- `lib/app/update_gate.dart` now exposes `bannerModeProvider`, read from the server's `app_config.banner_mode`: `per_screen` (the default) or `shell`.

## Part A: reserved bands (ships for everyone, `per_screen`)
- Once a surface is eligible for a banner, it reserves the band from the first frame. Eligible means all of these hold:
  - the build has a unit;
  - the capabilities say banners are on;
  - the player has no Quiet Pass / ads-off;
  - the capabilities are already loaded. If they aren't loaded, keep today's behaviour and reserve nothing until they are.
- The band height is the standard banner (50 dp) plus the existing gap/clearance tokens.
- If the ad fails or has no fill, the band stays reserved. Draw it as a quiet surface (a surface token, no text, no fake ad) so the layout never changes. If the player's ad-free state flips on mid-screen, the band may collapse with a short eased animation (token duration) instead of snapping.
- Keep the `belowPrimaryAction` clearance.
- Web keeps its current house banner.

## Part B: one shell banner (`banner_mode == shell`)
- A `ShellBannerHost` sits above the router, next to the other gates in `lib/app/app.dart`.
  - The host owns ONE BannerAd for the session. It loads once and is reused across routes.
  - It lays out as `Column[Expanded(router), band]`, so bottom sheets and dialogs stay above the band and the ad never overlaps content (AdMob policy).
- When the current route is one of the allowlisted waiting surfaces, the band shows. On any other route the band is removed:
  - every in-match route, offline or online: distribution, night, day, vote, result;
  - every pass-the-phone screen;
  - while the keyboard is open (`viewInsets.bottom > 0`).
- In shell mode `WaitingBanner` renders nothing, because the host already shortens the body. In `per_screen` mode, Part A applies.
- The shell unit id comes from a new `BannerPlacement.shell`. It uses a constant that falls back to the home unit until the owner creates a dedicated AdMob unit. Leave a clearly named TODO constant, and do NOT invent an ad unit id.
- Hiding and showing the band must not reload the ad. Refresh follows the AdMob unit's own refresh, with no manual reload loop. Dispose the ad on app detach.
- Doc 05: the band is never present during a match, so it can't differ by seat or role. Assert that in a test.

## Tests
- Part A: with a fake ad that fills after a delay, the content above the band has the same Rect before and after the fill, on each allowlisted surface (at least home, profile, lobby, online hub). Also check a no-fill case and the ad-free collapse.
- Part B:
  - The host shows the band on Home.
  - Navigating to History and back does not create a new BannerAd (count loads through a fake).
  - The band disappears on a match route and when the keyboard is open.
  - A bottom sheet's rect stays above the band.
- `banner_placement_test` stays green; update its allowlist only for the new host file.
- No overflow at 360×640, 390×844, 640×360 with the band reserved.
