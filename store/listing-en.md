## App name

## Short description
Play Mafia together: pass one phone or join an online room with optional voice.

## Full description
Mafia Master runs the hidden-role party game so everyone can play.

PASS ONE PHONE
Set up your group, choose roles and rules, then pass the phone. Each player privately sees their card and takes their turn. The app guides night actions, discussion, voting and the result.

PLAY ONLINE
Create a private room, share an invite or code, or browse public rooms. Set room rules before starting. Everyone uses their own device, with optional voice chat. Microphone permission is not required to play.

MORE TO DISCUSS
Optional morning clues and confrontations are based on match events. Use private whispers when enabled. Eliminated players can watch and make predictions; full match analysis is available after the game.

YOUR GROUP, YOUR RULES
Choose role counts, discussion style, turn times and optional information features. Local play supports 5–20 players; online rooms support 5–15.

ARABIC AND ENGLISH
Arabic-first layouts with English available. Profiles use a display name and avatar choice; online authentication is anonymous, without an email or password screen.

SAFETY AND PRIVACY
There are no ads during a match. After an eligible match ends, you can optionally watch a rewarded ad to double the base completion reward, or skip it without losing the result or original reward. Earned coins unlock fair strategy content and have no cash value. A permanent extra scenario is available as an in-app purchase and can be used by the buyer's whole room. Online play processes game and communication data. Voice is encrypted in transit and may use relay servers. Use Privacy and safety to report abuse, block a player's messages and voice, or request deletion of online data. Online public rooms and voice are intended for adults 18+.

Play with people you trust. Online voice and text are user-generated content.
