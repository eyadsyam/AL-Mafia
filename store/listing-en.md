## App name
Mafia Master: Online Party

## Short description
Online Mafia party game with secret roles and voice chat, or pass one phone.

## Full description
Mafia Master is the hidden-role party game, run for you: the app guides night, discussion, voting and the result, so everyone at the table gets to play.

ONLINE MAFIA
Everyone plays on their own phone. Pick a public room from the list — each shows how many players are in, its capacity, and how many more the host needs to start — or create a private room and share its code or invite link. Voice chat is optional, and you can play without microphone permission.

PASS ONE PHONE
Add your group, choose roles and rules, and pass the phone. Each player privately sees their card and takes their turn; the app runs the rest.

DISCUSSION BUILT ON WHAT HAPPENED
Optional morning clues and confrontations come from the match's own events, and private whispers are available when the host enables them. Eliminated players can watch and make predictions, and a full match analysis appears at the end.

YOUR GROUP, YOUR RULES
Role counts, discussion style, turn times and optional features are yours to set. Local play supports 5–20 players; online rooms 5–15.

ARABIC AND ENGLISH
Arabic-first design with English available; switch language in Settings. Online play uses an anonymous identity with no email or password.

SAFETY AND PRIVACY
This version has no ads and no purchases. Coins are earned from completed online matches, unlock fair strategy content and have no cash value. Voice is encrypted in transit, may use relay servers, and is never recorded by the game. Use Privacy and safety to report abuse, block a player's messages and voice, or request deletion of your data. Public rooms and voice are intended for adults 18+.

Play with people you trust: online voice and text are created by players.
